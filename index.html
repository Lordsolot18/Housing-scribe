Read this to the client before you start

Before we begin, I’d like to explain how I take notes today.

With your permission, this tool will listen to our conversation and turn it into written notes. It helps me focus on you instead of my keyboard.

Only I and the staff working on your housing will see the notes. I’ll check and correct them before anything is saved. The recording is deleted once I approve the notes.

You can say no, and it won’t change the help you get in any way. You can also ask me to pause or stop at any time.

Is it okay with you if I use it today?
